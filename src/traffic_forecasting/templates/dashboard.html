1. 5-Step Forecasting Pipeline Architecture

Total Demand: \(y_t = y_{eMBB} + y_{URLLC} + y_{mMTC}\)

In accordance with the mid-semester presentation (acn_midsem.pdf), the forecasting pipeline uses recent historical traffic to predict the next hour of aggregate demand without data leakage to proactively drive network slice orchestration.

Step 1

Data Generation

60 days (1,440 hours) of synthetic eMBB, URLLC, and mMTC traffic (seed 42). Total demand equals the exact sum.

Step 2

Leakage-Safe Features

Six historical lags (1, 2, 3, 6, 12, 24 h) and two shifted rolling means (6, 24 h). Initial NaNs dropped.

Step 3

Chronological Split

80% training (1,132 hours) and 20% held-out test (284 hours). No random shuffling to respect temporal causality.

Step 4

Multi-Model Training

Random Forest (180 trees, depth 10), ARIMA(2,1,2), PyTorch LSTM, and Per-Slice models benchmarked against Naive Lag-1.

Step 5

Capacity Decision

Rule panel evaluating predicted next-hour load against operator threshold to generate "Add Capacity" advisory hints.
